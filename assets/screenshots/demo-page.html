Translate long-form English text inline on the page

Inline Local Translator scans common text blocks on supported websites and inserts a Chinese translation directly below the original content. This makes it easier to compare the source sentence and the translated result without opening another tab or copying text into a separate tool.

中文:Inline Local Translator 会扫描受支持网站上的常见正文块，并直接在原文下方插入中文翻译。这样你无需打开新标签页，也不用把文字复制到其他工具里，就能直接对照原文和译文。

It is designed for local workflows powered by Ollama, and it works especially well for pages that load additional content dynamically while you browse.
